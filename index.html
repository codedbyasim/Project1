Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

Can I Afford to Survive? – L.A. Edition

A cancer policy navigation simulator built for Hack4Hope to show how California policies, insurance decisions, and LA cost-of-living pressures shape survivorship outcomes.

Launch Simulator Explore Data Sources

1. Introduction

This project provides a hyper-localized, interactive policy simulator designed specifically for the Los Angeles healthcare landscape.

1.1 Purpose

Demonstrate how California state policies (Medi-Cal, CFRA, Paid Family Leave) and local barriers (housing costs, transit infrastructure) dictate cancer survival rates in LA County.

1.2 Scope

  • Journey: Symptom → Screening → Diagnosis → Treatment → Survivorship.
  • Policy layers: Medi-Cal vs. Private Insurance, PFL, CFRA.
  • Social determinants: Housing instability, transit barriers.
  • Advocacy bridges: Link failure points to current ACS CAN priorities.

4. Non-Functional Requirements

Educational Accuracy

California-specific programs are clearly labeled (e.g., SDI, Paid Family Leave) to distinguish them from federal programs.

Accessibility

Mobile-first responsive layout, high contrast modes, and Spanish toggle placeholder for future localization.

5. Data Sources

Sources powering the simulation logic.

  • ACS CAN California Legislative Priorities (biomarker testing, copay accumulators).
  • California Health Care Foundation (Medi-Cal access and wait times).
  • LA County Department of Public Health (cancer clusters by SPA).
  • HUD Fair Market Rents for Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale.
